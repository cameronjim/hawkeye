(`L_Int_Grotto`) and its boss `DA_Boss_CH03_Santa` (all made by script: `Tools/Editor/create_mission_data.py`,
`create_grotto.py`; the frontage and the inside's dressing are planned by `_grotto.py`, the interior's rooms, doors and elves
are `Tools/Interiors/Grotto.json`, built by `generate_interior.py`). Maps: `L_District_EastVillage` and `L_Int_Grotto`.

one a draft, flagged `bDraft`; `ch03_spotted` and the `beat_santa` objective are ours). Rules:
`claude-docs/gameplay-semantics.md`, "Chapter interiors", "Chapter sites", "Mission" (stealth objectives), "Trick arrows"

Social Club. For testing: `-HawkeyeChapter=DA_CH03_Grotto` or `hawkeye.StartChapter DA_CH03_Grotto`.

## Beats (as built, 2026-11-10)
1. The title card ("The Grotto" / "Somebody stole a sleigh.") over Kate on East 7th Street (the district's PlayerStart),
   about 200 m from the grotto. Her hook line ("A department store lost a sleigh..."). `find_sleigh`, "Find the stolen
   sleigh display", marked at the frontage.
2. The frontage on a side street: a red "photos" sign over a street door (a camera on it, no lettering in greybox),
   candy-striped posts either side, fake snow heaped at the wall, a garland with three bulbs. Within 6.5 m of the door
   `find_sleigh` completes ("'Santa's Grotto.' Photos twelve dollars."). `enter_grotto`, "Get into Santa's grotto without
   being seen", marked at the door.
3. In by the door ("[E] Enter [Santa's Grotto]"): the chapter carries on inside `L_Int_Grotto`. The grotto: the throne at the
   back, the queue's candy posts, fake snow; an elf (green tracksuit, red stripe, an elf hat with ears and a radio earpiece)
   walking the hall ("Elves with radios. Sure."). Every elf is one crew (`grotto`): one who sees her brings the rest.
4. The stockroom (east): two elves face each other talking; near them, unseen, she hears "He wants the sleigh gone by
   Friday..." / "Somebody'll buy a hot sleigh." On the shelf by them, the boomerang (2 arrows, slot 4): "Boomerang arrow. It
   comes back. That's the whole thing, it comes back."
5. The corridor: a guard with a candy cane stands at the workshop door looking down it at the grotto's archway. The
   alarm box on the grotto's west wall (a red lamp) trips only to the boomerang's blunt head: its buzz brings him round
   the archway to look, and the boomerang comes back to her hand (if she has turned away it comes back into her: "Ow. It
   came back.", a stagger and 2 damage, never her last health). Taken down from behind ("Naughty list.").
6. At the workshop door `enter_grotto` completes (unseen, or seen: below). `open_vault`, "Find out what's behind the
   workshop door": the keypad beside it ("[Open the workshop door]") opens it ("That's the sleigh. That's four sleighs.
   And a lot of watches."). Four sleighs along the walls, shelves of watches at the back.
7. Santa in the workshop: "Ho, ho- oh, no." Then the fight (`beat_santa`, "Deal with Santa"): two sack swings he
   telegraphs, an overhead slam to dodge, then winded for the finisher. Down: a short beat on him, "You're a mall Santa.
   You had one job and it was the opposite of this."
8. `leave_roof`, "Get out by the roof": the roof stairs in the workshop's back wall come out on the grotto building's roof;
   a few steps onto it complete the chapter. The end card: "Good grotto. Bad Santa. Home." Back to roaming.
9. After: 8 s later the video call from the farm (a phone call: Clint, Lila and Laura off screen, Kate; subtitles only, the
   call's record in Clint's thread). At Safehouse 1's door, a little target ornament on a post: `[Look]` (optional,
   unmarked, offered once the roof is done): "That's not mine. It's a little target..." / "...I'm keeping it." raises
   `found_ornament`.

Seen: the first elf to have her Alerted spots `enter_grotto` while it is current: its title becomes "Get to the workshop
door. They know you're here" and she says "Okay. They've seen me. New plan: the old plan, louder." It is a fight; nothing
fails. The keypad waits until no elf is Alerted.

## Rough edges for the owner
- Greybox throughout: the elves' hats, Santa's hat and beard, the sack, the sleighs and the throne are primitives; the sign
  has no lettering; nothing in the grotto animates.
- The roof stairs are a door in the workshop's back wall; there is no stair to climb.
- The keypad and the door are next to each other; the door answers nothing until the keypad has opened it.
- The boomerang's catch is "she looks at it as it comes back": there is no catch input or animation.
- Inside, the `enter_grotto` marker names the street door, which isn't in `L_Int_Grotto`; the HUD shows no marker
  there and the log warns once.
