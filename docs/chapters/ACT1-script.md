# Act 1 script: DRAFT

Every line here is a draft by Claude for Cameron to edit, cut or replace. The beats are
his (`docs/STORY.md`); the chapter split is in `ACT1.md`. Nothing here is final until he
says so. In the build these load from `Tools/Editor/create_narrative.py` and are tagged
`draft` so they can be found and swapped.

Voices, as the story sets them:
- **Kate** narrates her own heroics, mostly to Lucky. Fast, pleased with herself, honest
  the moment something goes wrong.
- **Clint** texts in lower case and short sentences. Tired, fond, never says so.
- **Tomas** is polite about all of it. Compliments her form. His knees hurt.
- **Lucky** says nothing and is not impressed.
