## Rough edges for the owner
- The fight is easy on Normal: five thugs went down in 14 s without a hit in the lap. Five is the count the street
  layout fits; a heavy, or a second gunner, would make it a fight. Counts and weapons are `_site.CREW`.
- The event lines queued one after another: arriving says four lines and the unlock (14.5 s), so the first parry's line
  usually played after the fight. Fixed 2026-11-10: the parry and first-foam lines are reactive (ahead of the arrival's
  story lines, dropped when more than 3 s late; gameplay-semantics.md, "Chapter events").
- The foam unlocks on arrival, not after the first parry as the script has it (a player who never parries would not
  get it).
- The sacks are keycard pickups spinning slowly over the kerb; there is no carry animation, and the table takes them
  all at once.
- The police, the volunteer and the courier are greybox figures (cylinders and a sphere); the cruiser is boxes.
  Nothing animates; the officers do not react to Kate.
- The examine close-ups were moved up and to the side because Kate stood in front of the camera; check the framing in
  play.
- The banner has no lettering (no text rendering in greybox).
- The site stays on the district after the chapter until another chapter starts (the courier can be examined late).
