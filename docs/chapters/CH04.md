# CH04: The Social Club

Data asset: `DA_CH04_SocialClub`, its boss `DA_Boss_CH04_Tomas` (both made by script:
`Tools/Editor/create_mission_data.py`, `create_boss.py`; the roof is picked and laid out by `_arena.py`). Map:
`L_District_EastVillage`. Playable: Kate. No partner, no switching, no chase, no flashback. Lines:
`ACT1-script.md`, chapter 4 (every one a draft, flagged `bDraft`). Rules: `claude-docs/gameplay-semantics.md`,
"Bosses". Status: playable greybox, scripted lap passes.

How to reach it: it follows Night Shift in the campaign's order (`DA_CH01_NightShift.NextChapter`) while chapters 2
and 3 do not exist. Finish Night Shift, then the safehouse's Chapter select lists "4. The Social Club". For testing:
`-HawkeyeChapter=DA_CH04_SocialClub` on the command line, or `hawkeye.StartChapter DA_CH04_SocialClub` in the
console.

## Beats (as built, 2026-11-10)
1. The title card ("The Social Club" / "Tomas would like a word. Then a fight.") over Kate on the street under the
   club's roof, looking up at its parapet anchor. Objective `meet_tomas`, "Go to the social club roof".
2. One grapple up. On the roof: string lights on four poles, a speaker glowing purple behind Tomas, two folding
   tables, an inflatable Santa tethered at one side. Tomas (bigger than his guys, green tracksuit, no mask) and four
   of his crew stand and wait. The pre-fight talk plays (7 lines, about 25 s); the fight starts when it ends, or the
   moment she hits one of the crew.
3. Phase 1, `beat_crew` ("Deal with Tomas's guys"): two bats, fists and a shield. Tomas watches, out of the fight,
   and comments (a bat swing: "Is that load-bearing?"; a parry; one of them going down).
4. Phase 2, `beat_tomas` ("Deal with Tomas"): a jab, a jab, a slow haymaker, then he stops to breathe ("The stairs up
   here, bro"): 3 s with the finisher open and hits doing 1.5 times. He apologises when he lands one and asks if she
   does yoga when she dodges.
5. Phase 3, `survive_santa` ("Don't get flattened by Santa"), under 40% of his health: the Santa comes loose and
   sweeps the roof on a lane through the middle, the lane lighting up 1.5 s before each sweep. Three confetti arrows
   ("It's tactical"). Tomas keeps fighting.
6. Tomas down ("Good try, me"), the after lines (dinner, the cabbage rolls, the cruiser, the Sloan money, the city),
   then the end card ("Sloan money. That's Mom's. ...Not tonight.") and back to roaming with "[Chapter complete]".

## Rough edges for the owner
- Tomas is easy to finish: every jab he throws can be parried, and a parry opens a finisher (60 damage). The lap
  wins mostly on parries; his winded window came once. Fewer parryable swings, or a parry that only staggers him,
  is the knob.
- He reads near-black at night (bottle green); the crew are the stock red tracksuits with no holiday props
  (no candy-cane bat, present shield or light-string whip yet).
- The Santa's lane strip blooms orange rather than red; the Santa never hits Tomas and passes through props.
- The cruiser is in the lines only; Lucky is not there.
- The approach is one grapple from a fixed street stand; walking up the club's stairs is not a thing.
- Barks are counted per fight in the subsystem; a load clears them, so a line can come again after a checkpoint.
