# CH04: The Social Club

Data asset: `DA_CH04_SocialClub`, its boss `DA_Boss_CH04_Tomas` (both made by script:
`Tools/Editor/create_mission_data.py`, `create_boss.py`; the roof is picked and laid out by `_arena.py`). Map:
`L_District_EastVillage`. Playable: Kate. No partner, no switching, no chase, no flashback. Lines:
`ACT1-script.md`, chapter 4 (every one a draft, flagged `bDraft`). Rules: `claude-docs/gameplay-semantics.md`,
"Bosses". Status: playable greybox, second pass (solves, beats), scripted lap passes.

How to reach it: it follows The Grotto in the campaign's order (`DA_CH03_Grotto.NextChapter`; it followed Reindeer Games
before chapter 3 existed). Finish The Grotto, then the safehouse's Chapter select lists "4. The Social Club". It starts
with the standard arrows, the grapple, 2 foam and 2 boomerangs (chapters 2 and 3's unlocks). For testing:
`-HawkeyeChapter=DA_CH04_SocialClub` on the command line, or `hawkeye.StartChapter DA_CH04_SocialClub` in the
console.

## Beats (as built, 2026-11-10)
1. The title card ("The Social Club" / "Tomas would like a word. Then a fight.") over Kate on the street under the
   club's roof, looking up at its parapet anchor. Objective `meet_tomas`, "Go to the social club roof".
2. One grapple up. On the roof: string lights on four poles, a speaker glowing purple behind Tomas, two folding
   tables, an inflatable Santa tethered at one side. Tomas (bigger than his guys, green tracksuit, no mask) and four
   of his crew stand and wait. The pre-fight talk plays (7 lines, about 25 s); the fight starts when it ends, or the
   moment she hits one of the crew.
3. Phase 1, `beat_crew` ("Deal with Tomas's guys"): two candy canes (the bats), fists and a shield. Tomas watches,
   out of the fight, and comments (a cane swing: "Is that load-bearing?"; a parry; one of them going down).
4. The beat into Tomas's phase (2.5 s, skippable with Space / A): her input held, nobody hurt, the camera cut over her
   shoulder onto the two of them, "Okay. Okay. My turn, I guess." and "Just so you know, the knees are not what they
   were."
5. Phase 2, `beat_tomas` ("Deal with Tomas"): a jab, a jab, a slow haymaker. The jabs can be parried (he staggers
   0.6 s; a parry never opens a finisher on him). The haymaker is unblockable: a yellow "!!" flashing over him with
   the dodge key, so she dodges it. Then he stops to breathe ("The stairs up here, bro"): 3 s, the only time the
   finisher takes him, hits doing 1.5 times. Outside that window he guards: hits land at 0.2. He apologises when he
   lands one and asks if she does yoga when she dodges.
6. The beat into the Santa phase (3 s, a high angle over the roof): "Nobody tied down the Santa?" / "That was Ivan's
   job."
7. Phase 3, `survive_santa` ("Don't get flattened by Santa"), under 40% of his health: the Santa comes loose and
   sweeps the roof on a lane through the middle, the lane lighting up red 1.5 s before each sweep, shoving the three
   presents in its way along the lane. The solves: stand across the lane from Tomas while it telegraphs and he walks
   into it (the sweep winds him); the confetti arrow ("It's tactical") breaks his guard and winds him at once; a putty
   arrow on the Santa or its tether pins it 4 s. Tomas keeps fighting with the same combo.
8. The blow that puts him down: time slows (0.25 for 0.8 s) and the camera pushes in; then a 2.5 s beat on him
   ("Good try, me"), the after lines (dinner, the cabbage rolls, the cruiser, the Sloan money, the city), then the end
   card ("Sloan money. That's Mom's. ...Not tonight.") and back to roaming with "[Chapter complete]". A finisher that
   takes him from the Tomas phase into the Santa phase gets the same slow, close camera.

## Rough edges for the owner
- The lap takes every finisher the moment his window opens, so it wastes most of the 3 s. A player who hits him
  through the window (1.5 times) and finishes at the end will beat him well inside the lap's 94 to 99 s. Health
  (680), the finisher's share (0.05) and the guard (0.2) are the knobs, all in `create_boss.py`.
- The lap's fighting time sits near the 90 s floor of the target (three runs: 93.6, 93.8, 98.9 s).
- The lure (2026-11-10): the lane was 170 cm either side, and Tomas, closing to 120 cm of a Kate 110 cm outside it,
  stood on its edge; it caught him in one lap run of four. The lane's half width now comes from his reach (240 cm:
  his close distance, his longest lunge, her capsule and 25), and the lap stands her just outside it and asserts the
  sweep catches him at least once.
- Beats: the Tomas beat backs off to a wide shot when they stand far apart (both small in frame); the defeat beat
  framed Kate and the Santa and lost Tomas on the floor in the run I looked at.
- His rim light makes the bottle-green suit read, but bright and slightly lurid; his head glows orange while he
  telegraphs (the existing telegraph emissive), which the light makes louder.
- The heavy keeps his smoked-grey shield: it is a C++ slab with no mesh slot, so no present shield yet. The candy
  canes are greybox: red and white bands and a hook.
- The cruiser is in the lines only; Lucky is not there.
- The approach is one grapple from a fixed street stand; walking up the club's stairs is not a thing.
