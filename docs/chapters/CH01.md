# CH01: Night Shift

Data asset: `DA_CH01_NightShift`, its chase `DA_Chase_CH01_NightShift` (both made by script:
`Tools/Editor/create_mission_data.py`, `create_chase.py`). Map: `L_District_EastVillage` (game mode
`BP_GameMode_EastVillage`). Playable: Kate. No partner on screen and no switching (Act 1: Clint is a voice
on the phone). No flashback. Story: `docs/STORY.md`, Act 1; chapter split: `ACT1.md`; lines: `ACT1-script.md`
(every line in the build is a draft, flagged `bDraft` in data). Target play time: about a minute of chase,
then free roam.

## Purpose
The tutorial and the thesis, as a chase: the game opens mid-run after a bike thief across the rooftops, Kate
narrating to Lucky. It teaches sprint, vault, mantle, grapple, chain and the takedown in that order, by the
route, not by a checklist. A grapple goes wrong into a dumpster of wrapping paper; she still catches him.

## Beats (as built, 2026-11-10)
1. New Game: Kate is already sprinting on a roof (the route's start), the thief 11 m ahead; the title card
   ("Night Shift" / "Early December. The East Village.") sits over the first seconds of the run. Kate's
   opening line plays a second in.
2. Two 90 cm vent boxes on the run: a sprint vaults them ("See that? Textbook.").
3. A mantle up a level: onto a neighbour a step up where the district has one, else a 150 cm stair bulkhead
   on the roof (this route: the bulkhead).
4. The first gap: at her stand `chase_thief` completes and `cross_gap` ("Get across. Use the grapple") comes
   up with the grapple prompt line; he leaps it, she grapples it. Landing across completes `cross_gap`;
   `catch_thief` ("Catch him") is current, its marker on the thief.
5. A grapple and a chain: a zip at an anchor on the next roof and, half way along, a second press at another.
6. The authored gap over an alley: the grapple goes wrong. The line lets go, she drops into the dumpster
   (paper bursts up), sits in it a beat and climbs out into the alley: 3.1 s, the camera hers. The thief is
   put 16 m ahead. Lines: "Got it, got it...", "...Wrapping paper...", "You saw nothing. You're a dog."
7. He goes down the far roof's fire escape and along the sidewalk to where he is cornered, a few metres from
   the alley's mouth. "Lady, it's a bike!" / "It has streamers on it. You stole streamers."
8. The takedown from behind: "And that's the night shift." The end card carries her last line ("Hawkeye's
   sidekick." I caught him. Me. Put that in the paper.), then free roam with "[Chapter complete]".
9. After: Clint texts ("saw the news. nice work", "take a night off"), Kate's replies sit in his thread, and
   Mr. Pruitt the landlord.

## Tuning (on the data asset's `Tuning`, defaults in `FChaseTuning`)
Thief 620 cm/s on runs (her sprint is 700), 1.15x under 7 m, easing to 0.8x between 25 and 45 m; lost after
6 s at 45 m or more (the last checkpoint reloads); 16 m ahead after the beat; a load never puts him more
than 22 m ahead. He cannot be taken down until he is cornered.

## What the story removed from the old chapter (Rooftops)
The purple-fletched arrow examine, its close-up, the placeholder slides and the grey scene room are gone
from the chapter (the flashback and scene systems stay for later chapters). Clint's on-screen partner and
the switch key are off for Act 1. The old chapter's volumes, beacons, arrow and fighters stay in the district
as furniture, dark and quiet while Night Shift runs (removing them is a generator change for later).

## Rough edges for the owner
- The thief leaps every gap she grapples, from parapet to parapet; the first is 19 m. No believable gap
  shorter than that leads to a usable alley in this district; a plank, a lower roof or a different route
  shape is the owner's call.
- The thief is a Tracksuit thug model (the robbery runner's body); there is no bike-thief look yet.
- Lucky is talked to and never seen (no dog asset).
- The quiver is still the old chapter's test set (bow, standard, grapple, putty, bola, smoke, EMP).
